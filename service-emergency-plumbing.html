Emergency Situations We Handle

Is Your Situation an Emergency?

If you're experiencing any of the following, call us immediately. Every minute matters when it comes to water damage.

Burst or Frozen Pipes

Causes rapid flooding and structural damage. Requires immediate shutoff and repair.

Active Flooding

Basement or indoor flooding from any source needs emergency response now.

Sewage Backup

Raw sewage in your home is a serious health hazard. Do not wait — call immediately.

No Hot Water

Failed water heater especially in winter is an urgent situation — same-day service available.

Severe Clog / Overflow

Toilet or drain overflowing and can't be stopped? We'll clear it fast.

Visible Water Damage

Water stains appearing rapidly on walls or ceilings indicate an active hidden leak.

⚡ Stop Water Damage Now

If you have an active leak — locate your main shutoff valve and turn off the water supply to your home immediately. Then call us at (484) 860-9524 for emergency dispatch.
